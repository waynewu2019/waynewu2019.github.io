Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Symatics Technology Pte. Ltd. (“Symatics”, “we”) handles information when you visit this website.

This website does not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or embedded third-party beacons. If you contact us by email or through a link on this site, we receive the information you choose to send us: typically your name, email address, organization, and the content of your message.

How we use it

We use contact information solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate applications, discuss partnerships or investment, and — where requested — prepare non-disclosure agreements and technical briefs. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

Technical materials shared with us are handled as confidential. Where an NDA is in place, its terms govern. Unsolicited technical information is treated with care and reviewed by a minimal number of people.

Data retention

Correspondence is retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was sent, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time by emailing hello@symaticslab.com.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.
