Meet the Symatics team in Singapore this November.

SYMATICS is investigating a purpose-built architecture for stateful and reasoning-intensive AI workloads. This November we are presenting the architecture in Singapore — first as a public exhibit at AI for Good Summit, then in a closed-door briefing for technical leaders, partners and investors at NTU.

AI for Good Summit — exhibit

5–7 November 2026 · Shangri-La, Singapore

Visit the Symatics exhibit at the AI for Good Summit and follow the state path across it — MPU → SIF → fiber → MPU — the route state takes between nodes in our scale-out architecture. A fiber-spool control lengthens the link segment, and a fault button shows how the next epoch recovers clean.
