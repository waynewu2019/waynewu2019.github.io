Wayne Wu

Co-founder & CTO

25+ years in semiconductor design & engineering leadership

LinkedIn profile →

Wayne is a semiconductor industry veteran with over 25 years of experience, and co-founder and CTO of Symatics Technology, where he leads silicon engineering: SoC implementation, RTL and verification, emulation and FPGA validation, and high-speed interconnect.

He has led engineering organizations from early-stage architecture through IPO: early member of the ATI team through its acquisition by AMD in 2006, where as a senior project leader he was responsible for PCIe IP, multimedia SoC, GPU, CPU and APU products and contributed to Microsoft Xbox and Nintendo console platforms; GM of Atmel Shanghai; Senior R&D Director at Western Digital Shanghai; VP Engineering at Lightelligence, leading silicon engineering through the company's growth to its Hong Kong Stock Exchange IPO (HK:01879); and most recently VP SoC Design at eTopus, delivering PCIe Gen5/6, UCIe and high-speed SerDes programs. Earlier in his career he held SoC technology and design management roles in Hewlett-Packard's Fibre Channel Optical Communications Division.
